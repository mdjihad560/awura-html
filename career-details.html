About the Role

We’re looking for a passionate Software Engineer to join our growing team. In this role, you’ll design, develop, and implement innovative solutions that solve real-world business challenges. From building scalable applications to optimizing performance, you’ll have the opportunity to make a direct impact on global projects.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain high-quality, scalable software applications.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams (designers, developers, and project managers).
  • Write clean, efficient, and well-documented code.
  • Test, debug, and troubleshoot applications to ensure performance.
  • Stay up to date with the latest industry trends and technologies.

Required Skills & Qualifications

  • Proficiency in JavaScript, React, Node.js (or similar frameworks).
  • Solid understanding of APIs, databases, and system architecture.
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • 2+ years of experience in software development.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field (preferred).

Work Details

  • Competitive Salary:$70,000 – $90,000 per year
  • Location:Remote / New York Office
  • Job Type:Full-Time
  • Department:Engineering & Technology
  • Reports To:Project Manager / CTO

Why Join Us?

“Because your career deserves more than just a job.”  At our company, we don’t just write code — we build solutions that transform businesses. You’ll be part of a team that values innovation, collaboration, and your personal growth.

Application Process

  • 1. Submit your resume & portfolio.
  • 2. Initial HR screening.
  • 3. Technical assessment & coding challenge.
  • 4. Final interview with the team lead.
  • 5. Get your offer and start building the future with us!
